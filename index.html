Human Anatomy

Human anatomy is primarily the scientific study of the morphology of the human body. Gross anatomy (also called macroscopic anatomy, topographical anatomy, regional anatomy, or anthropotomy) is the study of anatomical structures that can be seen by the naked eye.

Toggle through different layers and overlays to see different biological systems the human body consists of. Finally, use the 'Magnifier' tool to get a close-up look of individual body parts.
